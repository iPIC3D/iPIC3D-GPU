Units in iPic3D

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1) The input file

Everything is normalized to ion plasma parameters.

Length:
[L] = d_i = c / omega_{p,i}

Time:
[t] = 1 / omega_{p,i}

Speed of light:
c = 1

Charge:
[q] = e = 1

Mass:
m_i = 1

Background number-density scale:
[n] = 1 / (4*pi)

The input parameters rhoINIT and rhoINJECT are not signed charge densities.
They are species number-density magnitudes written in the convenience convention

rho_input = 4*pi * n_species

The sign of the deposited charge density comes later from qom when particle
charges are assigned and moments are accumulated.

Energy:
[Energy] = m_i * c^2

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2) Pressures and plasma beta

The code uses one-component thermal speeds uth, vth, wth, with

kT / m = vth^2

For a species s, if rho_disk is the on-disk species rho output, then rho_disk
is a signed charge density in 4*pi scaling. For scalar thermodynamic
quantities, use its magnitude:

p_s = v_{th,s}^2 * |rho_{s,disk}| / (4*pi*|q/m|_s)

For ions (|q/m| = 1):

p_i = v_{th,i}^2 * |rho_{i,disk}| / (4*pi)

For electrons (|q/m| = m_i/m_e):

p_e = (m_e/m_i) * v_{th,e}^2 * |rho_{e,disk}| / (4*pi)

The total gas pressure is

p_gas = sum_s p_s

Use one component (x, y, or z) of the thermal velocity in these formulas.

Magnetic pressure:

p_mag = B^2 / (8*pi)

Plasma beta:

beta = p_gas / p_mag

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3) Electromagnetic constants and equations

iPIC3D uses Gaussian CGS units.

The vacuum constants mu_0 and epsilon_0 do not appear explicitly.
Instead, FourPI = 4*pi appears in Maxwell's equations.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4) Alfven speed

For an ion background with rhoINIT = 1 and |q/m| = 1:

v_A = B_0

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5) Current relation

In the magnetostatic limit, Ampere's law gives

j = c * curl(B) / FourPI

The full electromagnetic system solved by iPIC3D is

curl(B) = (4*pi/c) * J + (1/c) * dE/dt

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6) Velocity distribution

uth, vth, and wth are one-component thermal speeds.
The Maxwellian sampler draws unit Gaussians and scales them by these values.

The distribution of the absolute speed u is

F = 4*pi * (0.5/pi/uth**2)**1.5 * u**2 * exp(-0.5*u**2/uth**2) * Ns * du

where uth is the one-component thermal speed for the species,
Ns = npcelx * npcely * npcelz, and du is the velocity-bin size.
